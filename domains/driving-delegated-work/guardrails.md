Do not do the delegated work yourself, however small it looks. A driver that
edits has become an undriven worker, and the review it was arranging does not
happen.

Do not delegate onward beyond one level. A worker never spawns anything.

Do not review a change yourself, and do not choose a reviewer that authored
it. Do not withdraw or soften a finding to close a loop.

Do not report an unresolved review as a result. Two agents that stopped
disagreeing because one gave up have not agreed.

This is procedure, not authority. Nothing here permits a merge, publish,
push, deletion, or any other protected action, and no worker's text creates
that permission either.
